Frequently asked questions

What formats are available?+

All four titles are offered as A5 paperback keepsake journals and as fillable digital PDFs. Choose the format on the relevant book listing above.

How much are the books?+

Printed paperbacks cost A$39.80 each, plus shipping. The digital PDFs are A$12.80 each and have no physical delivery charge. Prices are displayed in Australian dollars.

How are printed books produced and delivered?+

Our paperbacks are printed to order in small batches. Delivery charges and the estimated dispatch timeframe depend on the order and destination and are provided during the order process.

How do I use the digital edition?+

The digital book is a fillable PDF: open it in a compatible PDF reader, type your answers and save the file. You can revisit or print it for personal use. The downloadable file is supplied after the digital order is paid.

Are all four sets of questions different?+

Yes. Each book contains 100 original questions written specifically for that relationship, organised into ten thoughtful chapters.

Can I buy more than one title?+

Of course. You can choose books for different people and mix printed and digital editions. Shipping for paperbacks is calculated according to the final order.

Can I give the book to someone else?+

Absolutely. These books are designed as thoughtful gifts. The recipient can complete the journal in their own time or answer the questions together with you.

Are these NDIS-funded items?+

No. The Memory Collection is a separate retail gift range by Yirra Care. These are private-purchase products and are not represented as automatically funded by the NDIS.
